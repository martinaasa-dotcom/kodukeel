# The 2026-10-31 reading, applied

The sheet was every line nobody had read, compiled from `main` at `8251380`: 14,561 Estonian, 18,232
Russian and 18,233 Ukrainian. The verdicts, one object per changed line with the reason, are in
`2026-10-31-review.json` beside this file. A line with no entry there was read and is right as it stands.

## How it went in

- **Russian and Ukrainian** through the translation sheet (`npm run apply:translation-review`): each
  correction written into the area file that holds its English, and every line, changed or not, recorded
  as confirmed in `docs/translations/confirmed.json`. 1,797 Russian and 1,537 Ukrainian corrections.
- **Estonian** by exact replacement inside the files each row names: the lesson, its scene's bank, picks
  and spec, the catalog scene, the public page or the table. A twin of a changed line in the same lesson
  (a model answer, an accepted answer, a fix row) changed with it. 645 fixes and 15 drops.
- **The workbook** through `npm run apply:workbook-review`: 126 kept, five refused into
  `lib/dict/refused.ts` in the reader's words. Four of its sentences were rewritten as the reader wrote
  them, in the dictionary data (`prisma/data/harvested.ts`, `expanded.json`) and the English table, on the
  operator's word; a re-harvest would bring Ekilex's own spelling back.

## What it took by hand

- **The dialogue in `b2-16-email`**: the mother's line about the textbooks moves into her turn before
  Tom's question, so the speakers alternate and her next line answers him.
- **`Tervitustega` / `Tom`**: the letter signs off with the name on its own line, a line break in the model.
- **Dropped**: the dialect lesson's `läksin metsa` / `läksin metsasse` row, and four refused phrases that are
  ordinary Estonian (`Kuidas ma saan aidata?`, `Kõik? Tubli!`, a bare `Tore!`, `tšau`).
- **The English the reading changed** (1988 to 1990 in Priit's story, the rumor lesson's `{Iga}` note, 161
  more): the Russian and Ukrainian follow the new English, retranslated from the reviewed versions and
  confirmed.
- **Words brought in before they were taught** (`siit`, `poetöötajad`, `põhjendatud`, `neljakümne`,
  `Kumma` and about fifty more): each is taught in the lesson where it first appears, or the nearest earlier
  one with room under the 24 an evening takes, with its English and both translations. A later lesson that
  taught the same word gave up its row, since a word is taught once.
- **Accepted answers** that the changed lines made identical were listed once, and an accepted answer the
  text no longer holds (`linnamuistenditeks`) came out.
- **Compounds and `-gi` forms** the reading wrote (`poetöötajad`, `meeskonnajuht`, `mõtlegi`) are not in
  the forms list, which stores neither. `isWrittenSpelling` in `lib/dict/forms.ts` reads them as the words
  they are built from, for the lessons test and `scripts/check-lesson-part.ts`.
- **Three C1 texts** came out one word under their part's floor, because joined compounds count as one
  word. The floors in `C1_READ` sit five under the round figures, and the reason is beside them.
- **Two English lines** from the reading hit the copy rules (`isn't just a party`, `don't worry`) and one
  ended on a reveal (`Not quite, Raivo.`); each was reworded in the fewest words, meaning kept.
- **`olemas`** is a word Ekilex has no headword for, so the scene bank's test reads the forms list for a
  catalog scene's line, as the route already does.
- **The dash**: Russian and Ukrainian write the tire as a spaced en dash. `withoutCyrillicTire` in
  `lib/copy/voice.ts` lets it through in a line holding Cyrillic and nowhere else; an em dash is still
  refused everywhere, and no line a reader sees holds one.
